# 📦 Supply Chain Transportation Analytics

This project focuses on analyzing transportation and shipment performance using real-world supply chain analytics techniques. The objective is to understand delivery delays, shipment efficiency, carrier performance, and cost trends through data-driven analysis.

The project simulates how transportation management and analytics teams monitor logistics operations and support decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

- Analyze shipment lifecycle data (planned vs actual)
- Identify delivery delays and SLA breaches
- Evaluate carrier and shipment mode performance
- Track transportation costs and operational KPIs
- Prepare clean datasets for reporting and dashboards

---

## 🧰 Tools & Technologies Used

- **Python** (Pandas, NumPy) – data cleaning & feature engineering  
- **SQL (SQLite)** – analytical queries and KPI calculations  
- **Power BI** – visualization and dashboarding  
- **Git & GitHub** – version control and project management
